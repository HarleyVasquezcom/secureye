TUNING AI ZONES: KILL 92% OF FALSE ALARMS.

BY R. VOSS · 2026-07-02 · AI · 6 MIN · 21 COMMENTS

Most “AI cameras” fail on default settings. Trees, headlights and cats become alarms. Here is the exact 4-step tune we run on every site.

1 · DRAW TIGHT POLYGONS

Cover gates + doors only. Exclude roads and swaying trees. Two small zones beat one big zone.

2 · SET MIN / MAX OBJECT SIZE

Calibrate with a person at the far edge. Reject anything smaller than a crouching adult.

3 · SCHEDULE SENSITIVITY

Day 60% / night 85%, with headlight filter on street-facing cams. Test with playback, not live.

RESULT: logistics depot: 41 → 3 nightly events. Same cameras, new config.

4 · VERIFY WITH EXPORTS

Export a week of events. If a clip wouldn’t convince police, it shouldn’t page anyone.
